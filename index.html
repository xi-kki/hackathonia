HACKATHONIA

My name is Isaac. I build AI agents at Xi-kki, mostly for founders and small teams. Hackathons are where many of my best ideas got their first real deadline, and finding the right one always took longer than building for it.

The right hackathon isn't the biggest one.
It's the one that fits what you want to build.

So Hackathonia asks a few questions first: virtual or in person, where you can travel, what you like building and how soon you're free. Then it shows only the events that match, with deadlines, prizes and a direct link to sign up.
